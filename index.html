Welcome to Southcoates Car Sales

Southcoates Car Sales is an independent, family owned and run used car dealer in Hull. We started life as a service and MOT centre in the east of the city, looking after cars for the people of Hull for over 20 years, and moved into retailing cars just over five years ago. That workshop background is why every car we sell is inspected and prepared by our own technicians, and why the service centre is still on site for anything you need after you buy.

Whether you are looking for a first car, a practical family vehicle or an affordable daily driver, our ever-changing stock of 16 hand-picked used cars means there is always something new to see. Buying a car should be straightforward, transparent and stress-free.

Cookie Preferences

Cookies are small data files that websites store on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit them. They act like tiny pieces of information websites remember about you. Here are some key things to know about cookies:

  • They make websites work better: Cookies can help websites remember things you've done on previous visits.
  • They personalise your experience: Cookies can be used to show you content relevant to your interests and browsing habits.
  • They provide website analytics: Websites can use cookies to understand how visitors use their site, helping them improve your experience.
It's important to note:

Cookies typically don't store personal information that identifies you directly (like your name or email address).
